NMDFC Loan Schemes

Schemes implemented through TAMCO as the channelizing agency of the National Minorities Development & Finance Corporation

🏢

General Term Loan (GTL)

General term loan for starting or expanding business.

Loan Amount:Up to ₹30,00,000
Interest:6% p.a
Repayment:Up to 10 Years

Eligibility:

  • ✔ BC/MBC/DNC category
  • ✔ Income below ₹3,00,000
  • ✔ Age 18–55 years
Apply Now
👥

MCS Loan Scheme

Small loans for business activities and working capital.

Loan Amount:Up to ₹10,00,000
Interest:5% p.a
Repayment:3–5 Years

Eligibility:

  • ✔ Self Help Groups
  • ✔ Income below ₹3,00,000
  • ✔ Age 18–60 years
Apply Now
🛠

Virasat Loan

Financial support for artisans and traditional businesses.

Loan Amount:Up to ₹2,00,000
Interest:4% - 5%
Repayment:Up to 5 Years

Eligibility:

  • ✔ Artisan category
  • ✔ Income below ₹3,00,000
  • ✔ Business support
Apply Now
🎓

Education Loan

Support for professional and technical education.

Loan Amount:Up to ₹20,00,000
Interest:4% p.a
Repayment:After Course

Eligibility:

  • ✔ Student category
  • ✔ Admission proof required
  • ✔ Income below ₹3,00,000
Apply Now

📄 Application Process & Requirements

Required Documents

  • ✔ Aadhar Card & PAN Card
  • ✔ Category Certificate (BC/MBC/DNC)
  • ✔ Income Certificate
  • ✔ Bank Account Details & Passbook
  • ✔ Business Plan / Project Report
  • ✔ Property Documents (if applicable)
  • ✔ Passport size photographs
  • ✔ Address Proof

Application Process

1

Online Application

Fill and submit the online application form with basic details

2

Document Submission

Upload all required documents for verification

3

Field Verification

Verification by Junior Representative (JR)

4

Application Processing

Review and processing by TAMCO officials

5

Approval & Disbursement

Final approval and loan amount credited to bank
