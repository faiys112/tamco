📄 About Tamil Nadu Minorities Development Corporation

Tamil Nadu Minorities Development Corporation (TAMCO) was established in 1999 to promote economic and developmental activities for Backward Classes, Most Backward Classes, and Denotified Communities.

TAMCO provides financial assistance at concessional interest rates to support small businesses, entrepreneurship, and economic upliftment.

🎯 Objective

To promote economic development by providing financial assistance for income-generating activities.

To empower individuals through sustainable livelihood opportunities.

🏢 Organization

TAMCO functions under the Government of Tamil Nadu and works with financial institutions and banks to implement loan schemes.
