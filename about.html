📄 About Tamil Nadu Minorities Development Corporation

Tamil Nadu Minorities Development Corporation (TAMCO) was established in 1999 as a state-owned company under the Companies Act, 1956, with the primary objective of promoting economic and developmental activities for the benefit of Backward Classes, Most Backward Classes, and Denotified Communities in Tamil Nadu. The corporation has an authorized share capital of Rs. 30 crore and a paid-up capital of Rs. 12.27 crore.

TAMCO serves as the channelizing agency for the National Backward Classes Finance and Development Corporation (NBCFDC) and implements various loan schemes funded by both the Central and State Governments. The corporation provides financial assistance at concessional rates of interest to individuals and groups belonging to BC/MBC/DNC communities for establishing viable trade or business ventures, thereby contributing to their economic upliftment and social empowerment.

Over the years, TAMCO has successfully disbursed loans worth several hundred crores to thousands of beneficiaries across Tamil Nadu, helping them establish and expand their businesses in various sectors including small trade, agriculture, transport, handicrafts, and traditional arts. The corporation continues to play a vital role in the socio-economic development of minority communities in the state.

🎯 Mission & Vision

Mission

To promote economic and developmental activities for the benefit of Backward Classes, Most Backward Classes, and Denotified Communities and to assist them by providing loans at concessional interest rates for income-generating and viable economic ventures.

Vision

To be the premier financial institution dedicated to the economic empowerment and social upliftment of Backward Classes, Most Backward Classes, and Denotified Communities in Tamil Nadu through sustainable financial inclusion and entrepreneurship development.
