About TAMCO

Tamil Nadu Minorities Development Corporation (TAMCO) was established in 1999 as a state-owned company under the Companies Act, 1956. With an authorized share capital of ₹30 crore and paid-up capital of ₹12.27 crore, TAMCO serves as the channelizing agency for the National Backward Classes Finance and Development Corporation, providing financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries for their economic upliftment and business development.
