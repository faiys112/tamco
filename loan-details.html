General Eligibility Criteria


✔ Basic Eligibility


  • Applicant must belong to Backward Classes (BC), Most Backward Classes (MBC), or Denotified Communities (DNC)
  • Annual family income should be below ₹3,00,000
  • Age between 18 to 55 years (varies by scheme)
  • Resident of Tamil Nadu
  • Valid identity and address proof

✖ Ineligibility


  • Defaulters of previous loans from any government agency
  • Annual family income exceeding ₹3,00,000
  • Not belonging to BC/MBC/DNC categories
  • Incomplete or false documentation
  • Already availed similar benefit under other government schemes

Required Documents


Identity

  • Aadhar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Voter ID
Other

  • Income Certificate
  • Bank Details
  • Business Plan

Terms & Conditions

  • Loan subject to approval
  • Interest rates as per scheme
  • Repayment period fixed
  • Documents must be valid
  • Government rules apply

  4. Loan approval
  5. Amount disbursement
