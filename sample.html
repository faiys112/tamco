General Term Loan (GTL)

Under this Scheme, Loans are provided for carrying out Business, to start an Industry or to expand the already existing Business or Industry. For example :

✔ Small Trade / Agriculture Development
✔ Handicrafts & Traditional Work
✔ Industrial & Service Sector
✔ Purchase of Light Motor Vehicles
✔ Agriculture-related Activities

Individual Loan Scheme is implemented in two ways that is as Scheme I & Scheme II.


Details Scheme I Scheme II
